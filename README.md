# My Favorite Movie

## 3 Idiots (2009)

**Genre:** Comedy, Drama

**Director:** Rajkumar Hirani

### About the Movie

3 Idiots is an Indian comedy-drama film about three engineering students who experience college life, friendship, academic pressure, and the challenges of following their dreams.

### Why I Like This Movie

I like 3 Idiots because it teaches important lessons about education, friendship, and pursuing your passion. It reminds me that success is not only about grades but also about learning, enjoying life, and becoming a better person.
